# Electric Vehicle Market Performance & Insights

A data analytics project built in Tableau, based on a global electric vehicle (EV) market dataset (`ev_market_train.csv`, about 200,000 rows and 37 fields).

## What is in this project

- **Tableau workbook** with sheets, a dashboard and a story
- **Dataset** used for the analysis
- **Screenshots** of the dashboard and story

## Sheets built

- Sales by Category
- Sales by Region
- Rating by Category
- Range by Category
- Top 10 Manufacturers
- Battery share
- Production Map
- Sales Trend
- Heat Map (category by market region)
- KPI cards: Total Sales, Avg Range, Avg Rating

## Key insights

- **Total sales:** about 1.55 billion units of annual EV sales in the dataset.
- **Average range and rating:** average range is 324.5 km and average customer rating is about 4.09.
- **Regions are evenly spread:** all seven market regions sell roughly 217M to 222M units each, with Europe the lowest (about 217.5M).
- **Two-wheelers lead:** Two-Wheeler Scooters are the top-selling category in every region, at roughly 23.7M to 26.2M units per region.
- **Small vehicles sell most:** Hatchbacks, Compact SUVs and Sedans follow, while Electric Trucks, Pickup Trucks and Electric Buses sell far fewer units.
- **Battery mix:** NMC (33.8%) and LFP (32.0%) together make up about two-thirds of sales, followed by NCA (17.2%), Sodium-Ion (9.9%) and Solid-State (7.1%).
- **Sales over time:** annual sales stay in a fairly narrow band across the years shown, which suggests a stable market rather than rapid growth.
